2026-10-27 - PHP2Shell Weaponized After Japanese Real Estate Extortion

Research URL: https://socradar.io/blog/php2shell-weaponized-japanese-real-estate-extortion/

Key Points
- STRU has identified an exposed staging server (open directory, 3,800+ files) belonging to an extortion operator, using the handle kkkkevin777, that held a weaponized copy of the PHP2Shell reproducer package (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619), added on October 21.
- The extortion wave ran between October 9 and 11, 2026 and did not rely on PHP2Shell. After compromising a Japanese real estate services company, the operator used a stored SSH key and a database password reused across sites to pivot to nearly 20 other Japanese websites on the same shared hosting infrastructure.
- Victims were pressured with fake maintenance notices on public homepages, ransom notes on admin panels (deadline October 11, 2026, 04:00 JST), a cron job that kept restoring the pages, and a bulk-mailing script aimed at the initial victim's customers.
- Stolen data includes 16,061 member records with plaintext passwords, income and savings details, plus five SQL Server backups, 663 FoxPro files and 730 business emails from a Taiwanese company. Customer data also exposes email addresses on major Japanese telecom, banking and technology domains to phishing and credential stuffing.
- No saved output shows a successful PHP2Shell exploitation against a live target, so it appears to be the operator's next capability. The server also hosted a PowerShell C2 for a separate "finance line" operation, and the files point to a Simplified Chinese-speaking operator who scanned 3,913 Hong Kong and Taiwan IP ranges.
