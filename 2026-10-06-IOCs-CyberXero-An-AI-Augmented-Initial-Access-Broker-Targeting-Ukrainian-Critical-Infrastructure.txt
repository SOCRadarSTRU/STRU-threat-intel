2026-10-06 - CyberXero: An AI-Augmented Initial Access Broker Targeting Ukrainian Critical Infrastructure

Research URL: https://socradar.io/blog/cyberxero-ai-iab-ukraine-critical-infrastructure/

Key Points
- STRU has documented CyberXero, a Russian-speaking, financially motivated Initial Access Broker that pairs commodity offensive tooling with an AI orchestration layer, allowing a single operator to run campaigns at a scale that would normally require a team.
- The whole operation surfaced from an exposed open directory (90,000+ files) serving the actor's live working directory, including AI session logs, scripts with plaintext tokens and exfiltrated victim data. Eight infrastructure nodes have been mapped across three ASNs.
- The adversary runs two parallel pipelines: a global, automated campaign against WordPress and e-commerce platforms (429 admin panels confirmed and 32 shells deployed in a 61-second window), and a curated, manual campaign against Ukrainian energy and critical infrastructure.
- The AI layer relies on up to 51 specialized Claude Code agents plus a PentAGI deployment wired into a Cobalt Strike Team Server. The actor's own session logs record model refusals and the context-engineering and session-reset techniques used to work around them.
- More than 40 organizations have been affected worldwide, with Ukraine as the primary target: over 628,000 Ukrainian individuals have confirmed data in the actor's possession, including 564,073 subscriber records from a district heating provider in Kharkiv. Poland, China and Pakistan were also targeted.
